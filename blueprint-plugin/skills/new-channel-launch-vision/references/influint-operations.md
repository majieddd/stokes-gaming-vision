## Access routes

1. Discover connected Influint tools by their actual metadata. Do not invent tool names or assume a connector is mounted.
2. If an authorized local pipeline is supplied, inspect its current instructions and commands. Read-only research does not authorize ingestion, ledger remeasurement, human signatures or publication.
3. The public MCP connector is `yt-intelligence-mcp.mjs`, Node 18+, with `YT_INTELLIGENCE_URL` and `YT_INTELLIGENCE_KEY`. Host/key must be supplied through a configured secure route. The GitHub Pages address is a static export, not an API host. Do not infer the host or paste secrets into project artifacts.
4. Without the authenticated route, use public intelligence, niche/standing pages, channel catalogues and per-video pages. Save retrieval dates/hashes and label `access_mode: public-export`. Report unavailable private fields. Public research is useful without pretending it covers everything.

The 5 October blueprint authoring session used current public exports. It did not call an authenticated Influint API. The packaged skill does not auto-configure credentials or register a broken MCP server.

Public tools are in read categories (`orient`, `channels`, `claims`, `workflows`). The public interface cannot run an audit or change a standing. A new channel must not be represented as its parent merely to obtain the parent's metrics. Use the parent as a labeled reference and the actual target ID when one exists.

Do not guess a workflow ID from its human-facing label. The discovery register supplies IDs. Relevant workflows include video suggestion, creative outline/hook/segments, packaging concept/brief, production/post plan, per-video retention, cohort narrative and moment census. Query those that exist and apply, rather than issuing every available call indiscriminately.

## Modes and evidence

- Law can act as a workflow rule, but record whether it rests on repeated measurement or signed practitioner judgment.
- Candidate law acts as a prior with evidence and limits attached.
- Theory acts as a hypothesis to test.
- Warning acts as a prohibition within its stated scope.
- Rejected acts as do-not-repeat. Read whether rejection is empirical, a judgment, retirement/merge or a bundled claim with a still-valid subpart. Preserve that nuance rather than reversing every sentence mechanically.

Carry claim ID if supplied, wording or concise paraphrase, standing, measured state, application mode, units and unit noun, causal rung, grade, confirmed niche, source date, limits, contested/merged status and the decisions it affected. The public HTML may omit a stable claim ID; record the source URL and a local reference key without inventing a system ID.

Never equate correlations with interventions, counts of readings with independent channels, thumbnail pixel statistics with measured depiction/face effects, or practitioner preference with tested CTR. The system's documented measurement reach does not establish every claim its store contains.

## Use through the seven chapters

| Chapter | Research question | Artifact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ideation | Which repeatable promises and subjects have evidence? What loses its appeal in transfer? | Format portfolio, two-axis references, gaps and counterexamples |
| Packaging | What is the promise, composition and evidence? What may only be taste? | Actual source/adaptation pairs and packaging hypotheses |
| Creative | Which hooks/segments fit this channel and what moments support them? | Opening beats, format arcs, source-linked interpretations |
| Production | What runtime/cadence/production needs are supported and feasible? | Incremental budgets, lead times, dependencies, assumptions |
| Post-Production | What happened at supplied timestamps and relative to the relevant baseline? | Real curves/cases, observed/hypothesized/proposed-edit separation |
| Shorts Strategy | Which relevant Shorts work as standalone stories? Is conversion actually measured? | Source-linked format/production plans and tests |
| Data Analysis | Which numbers/bases/coverage did the decisions use? | Source registry, claim usage, metrics, confidence and gaps |

The gaming swap test and premise/escalation/rules intro guidance on the public niche page are theories. They can guide creative tests; do not label them measured laws. The public warning about AVD versus APV carries limited gaming replication and missing CTR data. Avoid ranking a long event VOD as a watchability winner just because its absolute AVD is high. The rejected universal claim that livestreams feed rather than cannibalize long-form must not become a launch guarantee.

## Source and numerical discipline

Preserve the provided views basis. Current public channel catalogue notes describe lifetime views divided by the median of nearby uploads by publish date. This is not first-28-day performance or a controlled causal estimate. Catalogue completeness and stream/premiere exclusions may differ by channel; retain the notes.

For owned/authorized retention, use supplied timestamps, transcripts/frames and channel-native comparisons. A new launch cannot inherit a parent channel's curve as its own. Without captions or frames, use inspect-this-timestamp wording instead of inventing what happened. A pre-0:30 moment should not be presented as a ranked moment-census finding when that workflow excludes it.

The complete launch playbook is assembled from several workflows plus user decisions; `channel_deliverable` alone does not generate 100 finished thumbnails or replace the format review. Record what was consulted and what it changed. Report the actual access and data limits at handoff.
